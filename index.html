WHAT IS DESIGN THINKING

Design thinking is a human-centered, iterative approach to creative problem-solving that focuses on user needs to generate innovative solutions. It involves a five-phase process: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test, where teams work to understand user needs, define the core problem, brainstorm solutions, build tangible prototypes, and get user feedback to refine the solution. The goal is to create solutions that are not only desirable for users but also technologically feasible and economically viable.

Key Characteristics Of Design Thinking

  • Human-centered: Puts the user at the forefront of the process to ensure solutions meet real human needs.
  • Iterative: The process is non-linear; teams can and should revisit earlier stages based on new insights.
  • Collaborative: It brings together people from different disciplines to foster creativity and diverse perspectives.
  • Solution-based: Instead of focusing solely on the problem, it concentrates on developing and testing potential solutions.
